Chapter 01
The Beginning

8 July 2025.

Honestly kahu toh mane ae divase khabar j nathi hati ke aa date ek divas aatli special bani jase.

Somewhere between our conversations, stupid jokes, random moments ane badhi nani nani vastu... tu dhime dhime mari life ma ek special person bani gai.

Koi ek big moment nathi hato. It was just all those little things that slowly became memories.

“Some people enter your life quietly... and before you realise it, they become a part of your story.”

And maybe that's why aa little world exist kare che. Kem ke ketlak loko apni story no part kyare bani gaya e apne khabar pan nathi padti.

And somehow...
tu mari story no ek important part bani gai. ⭐

Chapter 02
The Clash

Have story ma ek evo part pan aavyo je mane personally bilkul proud feel nathi karavto.

Ek time evo aavyo jyare hu tari sathe vaat karvanu bandh kari didhu. Ek-be divas nahi... ek-be week pan nahi...

Almost 3–4 months.

Ane hu aa mate excuses banava nathi mangto. Mane khabar che ke hu things better handle kari sakto hato.

So... I'm genuinely sorry.

Sorry for the distance.
Sorry for the silence.
Ane sorry jo aa badha time ma tane kyarey evu feel thayu hoy ke tu important nathi.

Because the truth is...
tu important hati. Tu che. 🤍

“Ketlik vaar stars clouds pachhad disappear thai jay che. It doesn't mean they stopped shining.”
Chapter 03
A New Beginning

And then came December.

Somehow apde fari vaat karvanu start karyu. Ane aa vakhat hu genuinely try karva mangto hato.

Try to bring back the conversations.
Try to bring back the stupid jokes.
Try to make things feel normal again.

And most importantly...
try to keep you in my life.

Things obviously magically perfect nathi thai gaya. Apde badhu ekdam pehla jevu instantly nathi kari sakya.

Pan I kept trying. Because some friendships are worth making an effort for.

Ane haa... December thi aaj sudhi apda vachche conflicts pan thaya che. Arguments pan thaya che. Ek bijane irritate pan karya che. 😂

Pan badha badhu hova chhata... we stayed.

Chapter 04
We're Still Here

Apde laugh karyu che. Argue karyu che. Misunderstand pan karyu che. Ghani badhi memories banavi che.

Badhu perfect nathi. Apde perfect pan nathi. Pan maybe friendship ni beauty pan aa j che.

And somehow... we're still here. ⭐

Mane future ma shu thase e khabar nathi. Ketla arguments thase e pan khabar nathi. Ketli random conversations thase e pan khabar nathi.

Pan hu hope karu chu ke aa friendship aavi j continue thay. We keep choosing to stay. We keep making memories.

Ane years pachi jyare aa badhu yaad kariye, tyare apne smile kariye ane kahiye... “Aapde ketla pagal hata.” 😂
